Anti IP Logger

Manifest V3 · 125-domain list · English and Turkish

Anti IP Logger blocks connections to known IP logger and link grabber domains such as Grabify and IPLogger, and warns when a page behaves like it is collecting your IP address (WebRTC dumps, hidden iframes, click-less form submits and similar signals). It is not a VPN, it does not hide your IP address, and it is not a network-wide filter. It runs fully locally: settings and counters stay on your device, with no account and no telemetry.

What it does not do

It does not hide your IP address, does not filter traffic outside the browser, does not block ads and does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The blocked page shows the target address and three decisions: allow once in this tab, allow the site, or go back.
